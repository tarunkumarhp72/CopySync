# CopySync design system

- Background: `#FAF9F6`; cards: `#FFFFFF`; cream: `#F2EBDD`; border: `#E8E4DC`.
- Text: `#292824` primary and `#77746C` secondary. Accent: `#737B5B`.
- Use a clean sans-serif face, 4px spacing scale, small 4-6px radii, and `shadow-sm` only.
- The experience is calm and practical: no gradients, glass effects, large illustrations, or decorative animation.
